# Jesu Fitness — PRD-Lite (Portfolio Demo)

## 1. Product Summary
- **Mode:** Portfolio / Pitch Demo (client-side SPA, zero backend overhead)
- **Brand:** Jesu Fitness — an honest, pure-iron neighborhood strength gym
- **Positioning:** No saunas, no gimmicks, no fluff. Dedicated floor with dumbbells, barbells, bench press, lat pulldown, leg/quad press, calf stations, treadmills, and core machines.
- **Primary Conversion:** Claim a 1-day Free Trial Pass via the interactive Enquiry Form (with instant Guest Pass card + pre-filled WhatsApp handoff).

## 2. Target Audience
- Local lifters, beginners, and serious regulars seeking disciplined, affordable strength training in INR.

## 3. Page Architecture (6 Sections + Footer)
1. **Fixed Minimal Navigation:** Brand mark, 4 section anchors (`Story`, `Equipment`, `About`, `Packages`), primary CTA (`Claim free trial`), top 2px scroll-progress hairline.
2. **Hero:** Full-bleed dark iron photograph with scroll parallax, bold display headline (`Just iron. Zero excuses.`), concise 1-sentence value prop, primary + secondary actions, and 4 grounded gym facts (Morning batch, Evening batch, Starting price, Focus).
3. **The Story:** Scroll-illuminated manifesto statements + honest founding philosophy paired with moody real lifter photography.
4. **The Iron Floor (Equipment / Offerings):** Interactive horizontal scroll-linked showcase rail + prev/next controls highlighting real gym gear (Dumbbells & Free Weights, Barbells & Bench Press, Lat Pulldown & Cable Station, Quad Press & Calf Machines, Treadmills & Conditioning).
5. **About the Gym:** Architectural split layout with real training floor photo and structured hairline key-value rows (Training Floor, Floor Guidance, Open Schedule, Transparent INR Plans).
6. **INR Packages:** 3 membership tiers (`Monthly`, `Quarterly`, `Annual`) + interactive `Gym Access` / `+ Personal Training` segmented toggle with spring-animated INR totals. Clicking any tier scrolls to Enquiry and pre-selects that exact plan.
7. **Smart Enquiry + Guest Trial Pass:** Name, Phone, Goal, Preferred Package, Preferred Batch. Deferred `:user-invalid` validation. Submitting reveals a tangible **Jesu Fitness Guest Pass** (pass ID, summary, one-click WhatsApp prefill, edit option).

## 4. Out of Scope
- Backend database, payment gateway, authentication/member portal, fabricated luxury amenities (sauna/pool/cafe), AI-generated imagery.
